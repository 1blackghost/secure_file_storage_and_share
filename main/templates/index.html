About Us

Our app allows you to securely encrypt and decrypt files, ensuring your data is safe during storage and while sharing with others. Whether you're storing sensitive documents or sharing files across the web, our encryption system ensures only authorized users can access your information.

Features

  • Advanced Encryption: End-to-end encryption for all files, ensuring privacy and security.
  • Easy File Upload & Download: Seamlessly upload and download encrypted files with ease.
  • File Sharing: Securely share encrypted files with anyone, ensuring they stay protected.
  • Cross-Platform: Access your encrypted files from any device, anywhere, anytime.
  • Decryption Keys: Share decryption keys securely with trusted recipients for safe file access.
  • File Backup: Automatically back up encrypted files to ensure data safety even in case of device failure.
